Emergent Friend is built around a simple idea:

03 How It Works

A simple touch.
A circle of support.

01   Trigger

Press the centre stone twice on your pendant.

02   On the way

Your pendant vibrates gently to let you know help is coming.

03   Alert

Your phone sends an instant location-based alert to your care circle.

04   Respond

A trusted friend nearby accepts the alert and comes to help.

05   Escalate if needed

If no one responds in time, the alert moves to family or emergency contacts.
