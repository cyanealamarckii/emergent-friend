02 Story

Living alone doesn't mean being alone.

Emergent Friend is built around a simple idea:

When you need someone, help should feel close.

03 How It Works

You are just one simple touch away from trusted help.

01   Trigger

If you need help, press the centre stone twice on your pendant.

Press once, then again within a few seconds. If there's no second press, it cancels itself, so an accidental touch never sends an alert.

02   Alert

Your phone sends an instant location-based alert to your friends via a companion app.

Your pendant connects to your phone by Bluetooth and sends the alert for you. There's nothing to tap or open, and nearby friends are notified straight away.

03   Respond

A trusted friend nearby accepts the alert and comes to help.

When they arrive, they tap “Arrived”. Your other friends can see the request has been accepted, so only one person comes at a time.

04   On the way

Your pendant vibrates gently to let you know help is coming.

05   Escalate if needed

If no one responds or arrives in time, the alert moves on to family or emergency contacts.
